# 🏐 My Favorite Anime: Haikyuu!!

![Haikyuu Poster]

## About
**Haikyuu!!** is a *sports anime* based on the manga by **Haruichi Furudate**. It follows **Shoyo Hinata**, a short but determined boy who dreams of becoming a great volleyball player, and the team of **Karasuno High School** as they chase a spot at the national tournament.

## Why I Like It
- The matches are exciting and full of energy
- Every character grows, including the ones on the bench
- It shows that teamwork matters more than raw talent
- The rivalries are intense but respectful

## Main Characters
1. **Shoyo Hinata**: the energetic decoy with incredible jumping ability
2. **Tobio Kageyama**: the genius setter with precise tosses
3. **Kei Tsukishima**: the sharp-minded middle blocker
4. **Koshi Sugawara**: the supportive setter and team's heart

## Theme
> Volleyball is a sport where no one can win alone, and every player's part counts.
